Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background painting for an original 2D side-scrolling metroidvania game, Santuario de las Raíces. Wide landscape 1536x1024. A vast underground ruined sanctuary, slender weathered stone arches in the middle distance, immense dark roots descending from the roof, bioluminescent turquoise moss, small warm amber lights in niches, layers of mist receding into a deep teal cavern. Hand-painted storybook gouache atmosphere, sophisticated restrained textures, melancholic and quietly magical. Side-on orthographic view, all elements distant scenery, dark quiet lower third to allow bright gameplay silhouettes, gentle cyan light in upper center. Palette midnight blue, desaturated teal, touches of amber. No characters, no enemies, no text, no UI, no logos, no foreground playable platforms. Original world design.
